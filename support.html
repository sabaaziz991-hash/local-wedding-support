Wendus Support

For support, use the public support form at github.com/sabaaziz991-hash/local-wedding-support/issues.

App Summary

Wendus is a local-first iOS app for private wedding operations. It manages named wedding periods, editable schedule events, guests, households, RSVP state, seating, QR check-in, privacy-preserving vendor export previews, Excel-compatible guest list CSV import/export, encrypted archives, and post-event retention choices.

No Account Required

The app does not require a login or guest account. Apple Review can open the app and test the core workflows immediately.

Common Support Topics

  • Local event data is stored on the device by default.
  • The master guest list can be exported as a CSV for spreadsheet editing and imported from a user-selected CSV file.
  • Venue mode works without a network service after seating and QR passes are prepared.
  • Camera permission is requested only when opening the QR scanner.
  • Post-event closeout can purge sensitive fields, keep aggregates, archive the event, or delete local event data.
